About ResumeForge

We believe everyone deserves a professional resume — for free. ResumeForge was built to make resume creation simple, fast, and beautiful.

Our Mission

ResumeForge was created with a simple mission: to help job seekers of all backgrounds create professional, impressive resumes without paying expensive fees or struggling with complicated software.

We are based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India, and we serve users from all over the world who want to put their best foot forward in their career journey.

Our Story

ResumeForge started as a personal project to solve a real problem: resume builders were either too expensive, too complicated, or required creating an account. We wanted something different — a tool that just works, right in your browser, with no signup required.

Today, ResumeForge helps thousands of job seekers build professional resumes every day. From fresh graduates applying for their first job to experienced professionals making a career change, ResumeForge is designed for everyone.
