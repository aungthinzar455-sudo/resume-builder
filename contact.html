Frequently Asked Questions

Is ResumeForge completely free?

Yes! ResumeForge is 100% free to use. There are no hidden charges, no premium plans, and no signup required. All features including PDF download are completely free.

Is my resume data saved anywhere?

No. All data you enter stays in your browser's memory only. We never store, collect, or transmit your personal resume information to any server. When you close the browser tab, the data is gone.

Can I use ResumeForge on mobile?

Yes, ResumeForge works on any modern browser including mobile browsers. However, for the best experience, we recommend using a desktop or laptop computer.

What format can I download my resume in?

You can download your resume as a high-quality PDF file, which is the most widely accepted format by employers and applicant tracking systems (ATS).

How many templates are available?

Currently ResumeForge offers 4 professional templates: Classic, Modern, Minimal, and Bold. Each template can be further customized with different accent colors and font sizes.
