Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to ResumeForge. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at resumeforge.netlify.app.

1. Information We Collect

ResumeForge is a client-side resume builder. All resume data you enter (name, email, work experience, education, skills, etc.) is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to or stored on our servers.

We may collect the following information automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

2. Google AdSense and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Improve and optimize our website
  • Understand how users interact with the site
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns

4. Data Storage

ResumeForge does not store your resume data on any server. All information you enter into the resume builder stays in your browser's memory and is deleted when you close the tab or browser. We do not have access to any personal information you enter into the resume form.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense – for displaying advertisements
  • Google Fonts – for typography
  • Cloudflare CDN – for loading PDF generation library

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing the use of your information.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our Contact page.
